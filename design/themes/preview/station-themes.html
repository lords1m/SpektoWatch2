Ferrite · Bahnhofsthemen

Five stations, five looks

Ferrite's default comes from the white tile hall of U-Bahnhof Paradestraße. Each theme below takes a different Berlin station as its source, with the same token slots, a station colour (Kennfarbe) instead of ink, its own spectral ramp and a decorative motif. Every phone shows the same screen so the looks compare directly.

U5

Museumsinsel

Max Dudler, 2021

A blue barrel vault with more than 6,600 points of light, after Schinkel's 1816 night sky for Die Zauberflöte. The chart canvas stays night even in light mode.
